# Boxes (decided 2026-11-15 from a /proto/boxes prototype)

Problem: printers print one badge at a time from the print queue. They need
named groups of participants they can print together, so a whole box of badges
goes to the printer at once and comes out ready to pack.

## Decision

A **box** is a named group in one event, for printing only. Admins create it
under the event's **Boxes** tab, and every printer assigned to the event sees
it.

- **One person, one box** (changed later on 2026-11-15; boxes first allowed
  overlaps). A box is a physical pile at the print desk, so a person in two
  boxes was always a mistake, and printers found it confusing. Each person is in
  at most one box, decided in this order:

  1. the box their badge was printed (or reserved) through;
  2. else a box they were **added by hand** to;
  3. else the **first box in the list** whose rule matches them and which hasn't
     **left them out** by hand.

  Admins order the list with up/down buttons (not drag and drop, for tablets):
  specific boxes at the top, broad ones below. A new box goes above the first
  catch-all, so it doesn't start empty. Adding someone by hand moves them out of
  any box they were hand-picked into, and is refused if their badge is locked to
  another box (unlock it first). Leaving someone out of a box sends them to the
  next matching box below. The admin view shows how many more a box's rule
  matches that are in other boxes, so a broad box doesn't quietly come up short.

- **Rule** = participant types and/or closed session only. A box can also be
  hand-picked only, with no rule. A rule with no criteria is a **catch-all**: it
  takes everyone not in a box above it.
- **No stage filter** (removed later on 2026-11-15). Stage is display only: the
  stage bar and counts show In review, In validation, Awaiting print, Printed,
  Archived and Returned, so printers can see a pile isn't complete yet. Status
  settles Printed, Archived (also the old Collected) and Returned; otherwise the
  current step does: a print step is Awaiting print, a validator's step In
  validation, and any other earlier step (the reviewer's) In review. Only people
  awaiting print can be printed.
- Membership is worked out on every request and never stored. Someone who
  registers, or changes type, joins or leaves rule boxes straight away. Only the
  hand edits (`BoxMember`, INCLUDE or EXCLUDE), the list order (`Box.position`)
  and print-time placements are stored.
- **Split** a box into smaller boxes by Invitation (one per parliament, plus "No
  invitation"), Participant type, Surname range (A–E … U–Z), or **Size**
  (batches of N in surname order). Each smaller box can be worked on its own.
  Invitation and type parts are keyed by id, so renaming an invitation or type
  doesn't misfile anyone.
- **Print-time locking.** Once a badge is printed through a box, it stays in
  that box, and in the smaller box it was printed in, even if the person stops
  matching the rule. A Size batch is frozen as a whole once any of it is
  printed: its unprinted members are **reserved** (a `BoxPlacement` with no
  `printedAt`) and become printed when they print. A placement keeps the person
  in that box whatever the list order or rules say. Admins can unlock a badge or
  release a reservation, or take the person out (which also unlocks it).
- **Changing a box's split** re-files its locked badges under the new split
  straight away (under Size, into new batches of their own), so switching back
  later doesn't revert anyone. The editor warns that physical badges need
  re-sorting.
- **Printing a box** (`/printer/boxes`): _Send box to printer_ opens one PDF
  holding every ready badge, front and back. Confirming marks them printed
  through the workflow engine one person at a time (`processParticipant` with
  `expectedStepId`), so emails and the audit trail match the print queue, and
  locks them to the box. Only people awaiting print at a step the printer is
  assigned to are ready; anyone printed by someone else in the meantime is
  skipped and counted. Printers only see box members of the participant types
  they're assigned to. Size batches are still cut over the whole box, so batch
  labels match the physical batches for everyone.
  - Each run prints at most 50 badges, to stay inside request time and PDF size
    limits. The toast reports the rest: "N more are ready. Run the box again for
    them."
  - **Before printing**, the dialog flags badges that would come out wrong: no
    photo (when the design has a photo), or no badge design for the type (the
    old fixed layout is used). _Leave them out_ drops them from this run; they
    stay ready in the box.
  - **After printing**, _Some didn't print_ swaps the preview for a numbered
    list (with PDF page numbers) to tick the badges that came out right, with a
    "Printed up to badge N" shortcut for a jam. Unticked people stay ready.
  - If marking stops partway (any error other than someone else moving the
    person), the run stops, the badges already marked are still locked to the
    box, and the toast says how many weren't marked.
- Boxes go to printers only, so there is no sharing setting. Before people reach
  print, a box still shows them (as waiting), so admins can build boxes early
  and watch them fill.

## Added later (2026-11-15)

- **Box sheet**: a PDF with a label for the outside of the box (box, smaller
  box, surname range, count) and a packing list to tick off. A split box with
  none open gets a label and list per smaller box. Like the box page, it lists
  only people awaiting print or printed; the others are counted, not named.
  Awaiting-print rows are grey with no tick box.
- **Test print**: the print dialog can print the run's first badge on its own to
  check alignment and card stock. Nothing is marked.
- **Needs fixing**: box pages flag unprinted people whose badge would come out
  wrong (no photo, or no badge design for the type), the same check as the print
  dialog, with a filter for just them. Printers only see it for people at their
  desk; admins see it for everyone not yet printed. Printers can add a missing
  photo from the row (the photo editor now adds one when there isn't one, and
  returns to the box).
- **Batch reprints** (the reprint queue printed through the same dialog): built,
  then removed on 2026-11-16 with the dispatcher role, which was the only source
  of reprint requests. Printers reprint one badge at a time from the print
  queue.

## Archive box (2026-11-16)

Admins archive a box once its badges are filed: **Archive N printed** on the box
(or the open smaller box) runs the Archive action through the workflow engine
for each printed member, so each gets an approval in their history.

- **Archived means printed and filed, nothing more.** Archived badges still pass
  the checkpoint, printers still find them in their queue and can reprint them,
  and printers see them as Printed on box pages and box sheets. Archiving by
  mistake or too early costs nothing at the door.
- **The step doesn't change.** Printing already moves people to Archive Request
  (that move is what stops two printers printing the same badge); archiving only
  changes the status. A status guard stops a stale page archiving twice.
- Members not printed yet are skipped and counted; archive the box again later.
- **Unarchive** puts them back to Printed. There's no workflow action for it, so
  it's recorded in the audit log only.
- A box whose members are all archived is tagged Archived in the admin list.

Rejected: keeping printed people at the print step until an admin archives them.
It reads naturally, but loses the double-print guard, and printers already see
printed people through their print approvals.

## Rejected

- **Overlapping boxes** (a person in every box whose rule matches; the first box
  to print takes the badge): the original design. Printers saw the same person
  ready in two boxes. Replaced by one person, one box.
- **A "catch-all" flag** instead of list order: only two levels, and PAP already
  needs three (closed session above MPs above everyone else).
- **An overlap exception per box**: boxes are print-only, and a badge sits in
  one pile. Admins who want to watch a group use participant filters and
  reports.
- **Stages in the rule**: built for validator and dispatcher boxes, which were
  dropped. In a print box it hid people who weren't ready yet, making a pile
  look complete, and with one box per person it would have moved people between
  boxes as they went through the workflow.

- **Boxes for validators and dispatchers** (_Approve box_, _Hand over box_,
  chosen per box under "Shared with"): built, then dropped on 2026-11-15.
  Batching only matters at the print desk; validators and dispatchers keep
  working from their own queues. With validators, it also raised whether boxes
  should split validation by level, which isn't worth the complexity.
- **Tiles** (grid of box cards and a side panel): a good overview, but it falls
  apart with many small boxes, and it's ad-hoc grouping, not saved boxes.
- **Board** (stage columns, drag boxes between them): one delegation shows up in
  several columns at once, and drag-and-drop is awkward on a tablet at the badge
  desk.
- **Queue** (work through boxes one after another): good for printers, but it
  hides the event as a whole. The combined design keeps its strength through
  smaller boxes.
- **Work orders** (a box as one unit moving through stages, with hand-offs
  between desks): the box stays stuck until every member is ready, and the desks
  work by person, not by box. The "N ready, M waiting" counts cover the need.
- **Library only** (rule boxes with no hand edits): real lists rarely match a
  rule exactly.
- **Delegation** wording, and splitting by **Session** or **Country**: PAP has
  one Invitation per parliament, so Invitation replaces delegation and country.
  Closed session is part of the rule, not a split.
- **Re-sorting printed badges live**: without locking, a printed badge could
  move to another box overnight and no longer match what's physically on the
  table.
